# Supported earbuds

QuickBuds knows every model in the HeyMelody and realme Link lists: 140 models in 143 entries.
Three models have two ids. The app shows only the features of your model.
Settings › Known issues lists the limits of your model.

QuickBuds works on any phone with Android 8 or later, not only on OnePlus, OPPO and realme phones.

**The system holds the earbuds.** On OnePlus, OPPO and realme phones (OxygenOS 12 and later, ColorOS,
realme UI), the system's own earbud settings can hold the control link. Then QuickBuds shows at most the battery.
Use Settings › Bluetooth › your earbuds for the rest.

A model is confirmed when its owner sends a model test from the app. To confirm yours, see
[REPORTING.md](./REPORTING.md).
