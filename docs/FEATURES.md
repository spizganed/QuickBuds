# Features

| Area | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Battery and wear** | Each bud and the case, live. When the earbuds give no case level, the app reads it from the Fast Pair stream or the Bluetooth advertisement. When another app holds the earbuds, the app reads the battery over the Fast Pair stream. |
| **Noise control** | Off, Noise cancelling (Low, Medium, High, Smart), Adaptive, Transparency. |
| **Sound** | Equalizer with presets, custom curves and bass boost. High-quality audio, 3D audio, low latency mode. On OPPO, OnePlus and realme phones, the phone makes 3D audio for earbuds that cannot. High-quality audio needs an LHDC or LDAC link on models that offer a codec list. The phone's Bluetooth settings choose the codec. The High-quality audio switch changes the bitrate and the sampling rate. |
| **Hearing** | A hearing test that tunes the sound to your ears, and a fit test of the ear-tip seal. |
| **Controls** | Gestures per bud, wear detection, dual or triple connection, find my earbuds, prompt volume, tap sensitivity, firmware version. On some realme models: find my phone, auto-answer calls, enhance voices, virtual bass. |
| **Per model** | QuickBuds reads the model and shows only what that model supports. Settings › Known issues lists the limits of your model and phone, and the app tells you once when your model has some. A switch on the feature-sync engine moves only when the earbuds confirm it. Noise control moves at the tap. A failed change shows a toast with the reason. |
| **Android** | Home screen widgets from 1×1 up: the battery first, then the noise control and switches after a double tap. A 1×1 widget steps through your noise control modes. The battery strip widget shows the left earbud, the case and the right earbud as three rings, in a row (3×1) or a column (1×3). Quick Settings tiles: noise control (a long press picks the mode), low latency, wind noise reduction, dual connection and open QuickBuds. Settings › Widgets lists all 6 widgets and opens the settings of each placed widget. A notification with the battery rings and up to 3 widget buttons. On Android 16, a status chip can show the earbud levels in the status bar. An 11-page setup guide. A game equalizer that follows the game in the foreground (needs Usage access). |
| **PC** | A tray icon that opens a quick panel with the battery and the controls, on Windows and Linux. |
| **Looks** | Material, QuickBuds, Pixel art and Glass styles. OLED Black, Classic Dark and White themes, wallpaper colours, your own accent and palettes. Glass has six backgrounds. The phone and its widgets have all four styles. The PC app has the first three. |
| **Feedback** | Settings › Feedback sends a description, a model test or a crash report with the log, without a GitHub account. The developer can answer a report, and the answer shows once in the app. |
| **Languages and updates** | 27 languages, switchable in the app. Updates come from GitHub releases. The PC app updates itself. |
